Lines Deliberately Excluded

The following are not included in the under-construction list, despite sometimes appearing in lists of "upcoming Mumbai Metro lines":

Line	Corridor	Current classification
8	CSIA → Navi Mumbai International Airport	Proposed / separate project
10	Gaimukh → Shivaji Chowk, Mira Road	Proposed
11	Wadala → CSMT	Proposed / planning
13	Shivaji Chowk → Virar	Proposed
14	Kanjurmarg → Badlapur	Proposed

MMRDA's Metro PIU list includes these corridors, but their inclusion in the project master plan does not mean that active construction is underway.

For example, Line 10 is listed by MMRDA as a 9.2 km Gaimukh–Shivaji Chowk corridor, but it should not be represented as a currently constructed line merely because a DPR or project entry exists.
